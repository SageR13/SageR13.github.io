Course Correct

ProblemInstructors must organize student drivers and manage stressed ones while teaching, watching traffic, and documenting the lesson — all at once, with no dedicated passenger-seat tool.

SolutionA guide for a guide: help instructors organize student drivers and manage stressed ones during live, on-the-road training — with less visual attention on the phone.

Role

Lead Product Designer / UI·UX

I led Course Correct as the product designer: ride-alongs in the passenger seat, the glance-first live view, swipe flags, and the parked-car write-up.
