02 · User Research

After talking with 130+ learners, they often couldn’t remember a vocabulary word in a real environment.

79.1% said language learning improved with a real-world object.

talkIT explored that object and vocabulary relationship in real-time.

In person

Conducting in-person interviews, we observed and received feedback to change three things.

I have difficulty remembering vocabulary because if you don't use words frequently you lose them.

04 · Final Design

Turn your environment into a language learning opportunity.

talkIT is a camera-first language app: scan what’s around you, learn the word, and keep talking about it with an AI teacher.

05 · Outcome

Object-led conversations held people
three times longer than flashcards.

3×

longer than flashcards

FlashcardsStatic drills. Felt like a test.

Object-ledA chat about what’s on the table.

The camera scan became the first screen.

What I learned

Situated learning changed how I wireframe. The camera scan had to be the first screen, not a gimmick. Next time I’d instrument session length per object type during the tests, not after — we knew 3× in hindsight. And I’d track the age mix as a panel from day one: that spread was the finding, and it should have been an audit trail.
