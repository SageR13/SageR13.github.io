01 · The Problem

Students aged 18 to 22 often have no one available to talk toso they talk to a mug, a plush, a toy — objects that hold their trust but never answer back.

02 · User Research

After talking with around 50 people

What they disliked

It is no longer part of my imagination. I want my toy to be something whose soul is given by me.

05 · Outcome

Giving a soul to objects people already have

29/30

already talked to an object

Pre-scannedFelt like a tech demo. Gone in minutes.

Self-authoredAlready theirs. They stayed in conversation.

Authorship is the onboarding.

What I learned

Companion design is authorship design. The relationship started in research — when people shaped the object before it spoke — and that rewired every screen after. Next time I’d log session length by condition instead of only hearing the drop-off. And I’d screen for people who already talk to objects, instead of discovering that in the first interview.
