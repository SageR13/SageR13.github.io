03  ·  Core Mechanic

Your trail is
your enemy.

Every move you make leaves a fading scent trail. Rats follow it. As it dissipates, their path shifts — forcing you to think three steps ahead, not just react.

Dynamic AI
Rats recalculate routes in real time as the scent trail changes — no scripted patrol paths.
Player Control
You can exploit the trail — lay decoys, double back, or sprint to force a trail gap.
Tension Loop
The scent system creates emergent tension without scripted setpieces — every run feels different.
Skill Expression
Casual players survive by reacting. Skilled players win by manipulating the trail deliberately.
Precision Play
Requires exact timing and spatial awareness — where you stand matters as much as when you move.
Scent Strategy
Encourages deliberate scent manipulation — players who think like a rat win.
Split-Second Calls
Balances long-term planning with rapid improvisation when a trail goes wrong.
Replayability
Because AI reacts dynamically, no two runs play out the same way — the game rewards mastery without feeling repetitive.
