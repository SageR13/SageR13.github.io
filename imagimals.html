03  ·  Process

Three versions.
One that works.

Three weeks of sawdust, solder, and iteration — shaping both the physical enclosure and the electronics inside it until they felt like one seamless object.

Physical Prototype

Modular wooden night light — Hall sensors, RGB LEDs, Arduino, embedded magnets

Raw form
Sanding
Magnet inlays
v1
Proof of Concept

Basic Arduino circuit on a breadboard with a loose wooden shell. Confirmed the Hall sensor logic worked but the form felt rough and unfinished.

v2
Enclosure Rethink

Cut and shaped a tighter enclosure. Embedded magnets more precisely. Electronics still exposed — needed a cleaner internal layout.

v3
Final Form

Soldered final circuit, routed wiring internally, and hand-finished the wood. The object felt intentional — both as a toy and as a night light.
