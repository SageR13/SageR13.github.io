01  ·  The Problem

Teaching driving is
high-stakes multitasking.

Driving instructors are expected to monitor student behavior, navigate live road conditions, provide real-time feedback, and track lesson progress — all simultaneously, with no dedicated tools to help.

Student Driver
Lessons progress through three stages: learning safe driving fundamentals, practicing turns on local roads, and advancing to highway driving — each with distinct skill demands.
Driving Instructor
"Once a student feels comfortable with roads and skills, I take them to an unfamiliar lot so they can carry that skill no matter the environment."
Industry Research
"Driving instructor stress is a real and common challenge. Balancing student safety, managing expectations, and adapting to live road conditions requires a high level of patience and skill."

02  ·  User Research

Method

Participants

Synthesis

Finding 01
Instructors continue to handwrite notes. Instructors log feedback during and after the lesson.
Finding 02
Driving instructor lose track of progress for each student over time, and have difficulty maintaining the skillset for each one
Design Opportunity
A non-distracting, voice-first logging tool that captures feedback in the moment and organizes it automatically per student built with a smart navigational interface.
Design Principle
Eyes stay on the road. Every interaction should require minimal attention.

05  ·  Process

Iteration.
Intention.

The interface went through significant iteration — from an information-dense dashboard that demanded too much attention, to a stripped-back, glance-first layout that keeps the instructor focused on the road.

v1
Full Dashboard

Detailed stats and controls on a single screen. Instructors found it overwhelming mid-lesson; too much information competing for attention while watching the road.

v2
Tabbed Layout

Separated live logging from student history. Reduced cognitive load per screen, but switching tabs mid-lesson still pulled focus; Navigation needed to feel more automatic.

v3
Glance-First Design

Reduced the live view to only what matters in the moment: map, large swipe targets, and one active flag. Everything else surfaces before/after the session.
