Scoring

Difficulty

04  ·  Design

Player
Engagement.

Unlockable content and a ranked competitive ladder keep both casual and hardcore players invested across dozens of sessions.

Casual Hook
Accessible one-finger swipe mechanic — playable in short sessions without prior rhythm game experience.
Competitive Layer
Ranked matchmaking and precision scoring reward mastery and keep skilled players challenged.
Progression
Unlockable band members, stages, and song tracks tied to story milestones and ranked achievements.
Replayability
Each song has three difficulty tiers. High-score leaderboards and daily challenges drive repeat play.
Design Goal
Make the base mechanic learnable in under 60 seconds — no tutorial needed for casual players.
Design Goal
Give competitive players a visible skill ceiling — timing precision scoring, combo multipliers, and ranked tiers.
Finding
Narrative framing increases session length — players continue to see what comes next beyond the core mechanic.
Finding
Character personality and animal theming creates emotional attachment that pure score-chasing alone cannot.
