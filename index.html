Problem

Many users rely on mirrors, smartphones, or low-quality tools to inspect their oral health at home, which often provides insufficient visibility and leads to uncertainty.

&

Solution

At-home smart dental camera controlled by tongue movements, paired with an app for analysis, documentation, and sharing.

User Research
"Hard to check areas like back tooth or throat"
"I have been confused about whether I should visit a doctor because I wasn't sure how serious the issue was"
"Even if I can see inside my mouth clearly, I would not be able to tell the problem"
"Hard to direct light in desired places"
"Ability to share with a doctor"

Raccoon!

Battle other animal groups as a raccoon garage-band in this rhythm mobile game!

Overview

game preview
Role: Team Lead, Product Designer, UI/UX Designer, Visual Artist, Co-creator
Team: 6 members
Duration: 11 weeks
  • Led product vision and development roadmap
  • Designed end-to-end UI/UX design system
  • Created splash art and concepts

Designing a rhythm game that is accessible to casual players while sustaining long-term engagement for competitive users.

Mechanics

  • Falling targets are destroyed by the player via tapping in time with the music
  • Points are awarded for better timing and multi-note combos
  • Difficulty can be adjusted by changing the speed, frequency, and location of beats

Narrative

A raccoon garage band is forced out of their home by rival cats and must battle animal bands across diverse music genres to reclaim their shelter and rise to the top.

Player Engagement

Tests rhythm, timing, and reflexes through fast-paced gameplay, while progressive difficulty and unlockable content encourage skill mastery and replayability.

A PC stealth-action game combining strategy and action elements.

Overview

Role: Design Lead, Co-creator
Team: 8 members
Duration: 13 weeks
  • Led product vision and development roadmap
  • Designed end-to-end UI/UX design system
  • Created splash art and concepts
  • Managed sprint planning and cross-functional alignment between designers and programmers

The game is a struggle of going against an opposing force, where one plays as a cookie in a challenging environment filled with hungry rats. It serves as an example of overcoming obstacles in real life, with quick and resourceful thinking.

Core Mechanic

Dynamic Scent Trails

Enemies follow the dissipating scent trail that Big Buddy and Little Buddy leave behind. The player controls both Big Buddy and Little Buddy, marking a path that attracts rats as the player navigates through levels. Unlike static trail marks, the scent trail fades over time, and the player needs to adapt their strategies accordingly.

Player Engagement

  • Requires precise timing, trail masking, and strategic positioning to avoid capture and deceive Rats.
  • Interacting with scent trails enables players to manipulate Rat behavior, emphasizing planning and execution.
