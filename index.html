Financial Services Redesign

Client: Major Banking Institution Year: 2024 Role: Lead UX Designer
UX Design Strategy Research
🎨

Challenge

A leading financial institution was struggling with declining user engagement and increasing customer support tickets. Their digital banking platform was outdated, difficult to navigate, and didn't meet modern user expectations.

Approach

We conducted comprehensive user research including:

  • 30+ user interviews across different demographics
  • Competitive analysis of top digital banking platforms
  • Usability testing of existing platform
  • Journey mapping to identify pain points

Solution

We redesigned the entire digital banking experience with a focus on intuitive navigation, personalized dashboards, and streamlined transaction flows. Key features included:

  • Smart financial insights powered by data visualization
  • One-tap bill payments and transfers
  • Personalized spending analytics
  • Enhanced security with biometric authentication

Results

  • 45% increase in user engagement
  • 30% reduction in customer support tickets
  • 4.8/5 average user satisfaction rating
  • 20% increase in mobile app adoption

Healthcare Innovation

Client: Regional Healthcare Network Year: 2024 Role: Service Design Lead
Service Design Healthcare Innovation
🚀

Challenge

A regional healthcare network faced high appointment no-show rates and accessibility challenges, particularly for patients in rural areas. The existing telehealth solution was underutilized and difficult to use.

Approach

We took a human-centered approach to understand the needs of both patients and healthcare providers:

  • Ethnographic research with patients and doctors
  • Service blueprinting to map the entire care journey
  • Co-creation workshops with stakeholders
  • Accessibility audits and compliance review

Solution

We designed a patient-centered telehealth platform that prioritizes accessibility and ease of use:

  • Simplified scheduling with automated reminders
  • One-click video consultations
  • Integrated prescription management
  • Multi-language support and accessibility features

Results

  • 60% reduction in appointment no-shows
  • 85% patient satisfaction score
  • 3x increase in telehealth adoption
  • Improved access for rural patients

Sustainable Supply Chain

Client: Fortune 500 Manufacturing Company Year: 2024 Role: Data Visualization Designer
Data Viz Sustainability Enterprise
💡

Challenge

A Fortune 500 manufacturing company needed to track and report ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) metrics across their global supply chain. Existing tools were fragmented and data was difficult to interpret.

Approach

We designed a comprehensive data visualization dashboard:

  • Stakeholder workshops to define key metrics
  • Information architecture for complex data sets
  • Visual design exploration for clarity and impact
  • Interactive prototyping and user testing

Solution

An enterprise dashboard that transforms complex sustainability data into actionable insights:

  • Real-time tracking of carbon emissions
  • Supply chain transparency visualization
  • Automated compliance reporting
  • Predictive analytics for sustainability goals

Results

  • 70% reduction in reporting time
  • Improved supplier accountability
  • Enhanced investor transparency
  • Achieved carbon neutrality targets ahead of schedule

Retail Experience Platform

Client: National Retail Chain Year: 2024 Role: CX Design Lead
Omnichannel Retail CX Design
🎯

Challenge

A national retail chain needed to create a seamless experience across their physical stores, website, and mobile app. Customers were frustrated by disconnected touchpoints and inconsistent experiences.

Approach

We designed an omnichannel experience strategy:

  • Customer journey mapping across all touchpoints
  • In-store ethnographic research
  • Digital experience audits
  • Service design workshops with staff

Solution

A unified platform that connects physical and digital experiences:

  • Scan-and-go mobile checkout
  • In-store product lookup and reviews
  • Buy online, pick up in store (BOPIS)
  • Personalized recommendations across channels

Results

  • 35% increase in customer satisfaction
  • 25% boost in cross-channel purchases
  • 50% reduction in checkout wait times
  • Improved inventory accuracy and management
