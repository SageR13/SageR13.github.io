confiDENT

An At-Home Intraoral Camera for Oral Health Monitoring

Final Product

3D Model Viewer

Loading 3D model...

Controls: Left-click + drag to rotate • Right-click + drag to pan • Scroll to zoom

Background

Many people struggle to inspect their oral health at home because common tools like mirrors or phone cameras provide unclear and unstable views. This lack of visibility often leads to uncertainty, anxiety, and delayed care. With rising interest in at-home health management, there is a growing need for simple, reliable ways for users to observe early changes before deciding whether to seek professional help.

Target Users

People (18-29) who want to monitor their oral or minor health concerns at home in the US, including individual users and families.

Current Situation

Many users rely on mirrors, smartphones, or low-quality tools to inspect their oral health at home, which often provides insufficient visibility and leads to uncertainty.

Improved Situation

Users can have a more proficient way to observe and assess their oral health to reduce anxiety and enable early detection before visiting a doctor.

Design Solution

Device

High-quality, detailed, multi-angle images for fast, effortless daily checkups

App

Capture, document, and connect - so professionals can ensure you receive timely care when it matters most

We focus on enhancing users' ability to clearly observe their oral health, not diagnosing or replacing professional medical evaluation. Our design prioritizes simplicity, accessibility, and emotional reassurance, supporting easy documentation and smooth communication with professionals.
