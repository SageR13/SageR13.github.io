Project Title One

UI/UX Design • 2024

Overview

This project focused on redesigning a mobile application to improve user engagement and streamline the checkout process. The goal was to create a seamless, intuitive experience that would increase conversion rates by 30%.

🎨

The Challenge

Users were abandoning their carts at a rate of 65%, primarily due to a confusing navigation system and a multi-step checkout process that felt overwhelming. The existing design hadn't been updated in three years and didn't reflect modern UX best practices.

The Solution

After conducting user interviews and analyzing heat maps, I redesigned the entire user flow. Key improvements included a simplified navigation structure, a one-page checkout system, and clear visual hierarchy throughout the app.

I created high-fidelity prototypes in Figma and conducted three rounds of user testing to validate the design decisions before handoff to development.

📊

Results

The redesign led to a 42% increase in completed checkouts and a 35% improvement in overall user satisfaction scores. The client reported the changes paid for themselves within the first two months of launch.
