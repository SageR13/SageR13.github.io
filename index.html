confiDENT - Intraoral Camera

Client: Dentists and Patients Year: 2025 Role: Product Designer
Product Design Healthcare 3D Modeling

Challenge

Dental professionals needed a more ergonomic and technologically advanced intraoral camera that would improve patient communication and diagnostic accuracy while being comfortable to use throughout long procedures.

Approach

We collaborated with dental professionals to understand their workflow and pain points:

  • Ergonomic studies with practicing dentists
  • Patient feedback on current camera experiences
  • 3D prototyping and iterative design refinement
  • Material selection for medical-grade safety

Solution

A next-generation intraoral camera featuring:

  • Ergonomic grip designed for extended use
  • High-resolution imaging for detailed diagnostics
  • Wireless connectivity for seamless integration
  • Autoclavable components for hygiene standards

Results

  • 95% practitioner satisfaction rating
  • 40% improvement in patient understanding
  • Reduced procedure time by 15%
  • Enhanced diagnostic accuracy
